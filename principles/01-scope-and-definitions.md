# 01 - Scope and Definitions

## 1.1 Purpose
This document establishes the primary definitions and scope for the Aether Navigation framework, operating under Scalar Relaxation Cosmology (SRC) and Dynamic Fluid Medium (DFM) mechanics. It explicitly rejects force-based gravitational paradigms in favor of medium-state navigation.

## 1.2 Key Definitions

- **Aether Substrate:** The history-preserving, flowing fluid medium that underpins spacetime phenomena, mathematically modeled via Riemann theta functions on finite-gap compact Riemann surfaces.
- **Gravitational Destination (Sink):** An energetic gradient resulting from the collective discharge of quantum-mechanical collective states residing within planetary and stellar cores. Gravity is the directional flow toward this sink, not an intrinsic pulling force between isolated point-masses.
- **Dynamic Fluid Medium (DFM):** The active, fluctuating substrate whose local density and adiabatic moduli dictate orbital capture, satellite transitions, and field propagation.
- **Neutral Collective Modes:** Quantum-mechanical macroscopic states within matter and cores that, when phase-synchronized, alter the local vacuum refractive index and coupling efficiency with the DFM.
- **Lorentz/Eddy Current Steering:** The modulation of plasma fields interfacing with core discharge channels to vector craft movement across medium density gradients.
- **Decoherence Control:** The induction of controlled local discharge cycles designed to modify an object's potential energy state relative to a primary body's sink, circumventing the need for brute-force reactive thrust.

## 1.3 Methodological Boundaries
This framework explicitly distinguishes between:
1. **Academic Principles:** The fundamental wave mechanics, aether properties, and core-discharge thermodynamics.
2. **Applied Systems:** The hardware architecture, plasma coupling arrays, and control electronics designed to interface with the principles.

All propositions within this repository are evaluated against explanatory depth, medium coherence, and empirical falsifiability rather than alignment with General Relativity or Standard Model consensus.
