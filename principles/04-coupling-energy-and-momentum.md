# 04 - Coupling Energy and Momentum

## 4.1 The Illusion of Closed-System Momentum
Conventional mechanics insists on strict local momentum conservation within a closed system, requiring equal and opposite reaction mass for every acceleration vector. Within the Dynamic Fluid Medium (DFM) framework, this constraint is an artifact of treating the local vacuum as an inert, empty backdrop.

When a system operates inside a flowing, history-preserving aether substrate, it is an **open thermodynamic and hydrodynamic system**. Momentum is not created ex nihilo, nor is it conserved solely within the visible craft-mass boundary; it is exchanged continuously with the aether substrate via field-coupling.

## 4.2 Lorentz and Eddy Current Coupling
Energy and momentum transfer between a vehicle and the DFM are mediated through structured electromagnetic and plasma interactions. By establishing controlled **Lorentz and Eddy currents** within the craft's primary boundary containment fields, the system interfaces directly with the plasma channels anchoring the planetary core's discharge.

- **Field Vectoring:** Rather than pushing against ambient gas or ejecting reaction mass, the craft's internal coil and plasma arrays generate localized magnetic topology shifts.
- **Medium Adhesion:** These topologies alter the local skin friction against the flowing aether, allowing the craft to "grab" or "release" specific streamlines of the DFM.
- **Vector Control:** Steering is achieved by asymmetrical modulation of these plasma boundaries, effectively vectoring the craft along the natural pressure and density gradients of the substrate.

## 4.3 Potential Energy Shifting via Decoherence Cycles
True medium navigation does not rely on kinetic acceleration (increasing velocity via brute-force force application). Instead, it alters the object's **potential energy state** relative to the primary gravitational sink.

- **Sink Gradient Positioning:** Gravitational attraction is a directional flow toward a core's energetic sink. By inducing controlled **decoherence cycles** in the craft's internal neutral collective modes, the system temporarily detunes its coupling constant with the primary sink.
- **Potential Wells:** This detuning effectively lifts or drops the craft into a different potential well of the DFM.
- **Inertial Anistropy:** Occupants and internal structures experience zero g-forces during these transitions because the entire local control volume shifts its baseline metric relative to the aether flow simultaneously.

## 4.4 Conservation Laws in an Open Substrate
Energy expended by the craft's control systems does not vanish; it is injected back into or extracted from the local aether substrate as modified pulse structures and phase shifts. The total energy equation balances across the macro-system (Craft + DFM + Core Sink), resolving apparent anomalies in conservation laws by recognizing that the "vacuum" is an active energy reservoir capable of dynamic work storage and release.
