# 05 - Testable Predictions and Falsifiability

## 5.1 Purpose
A speculative framework remains unscientific if it cannot be falsified or if it hides behind unfalsifiable abstractions. This document outlines concrete, testable predictions derived from Scalar Relaxation Cosmology (SRC) and Dynamic Fluid Medium (DFM) mechanics, establishing empirical anchors for aether navigation.

## 5.2 Anomalies in High-Precision Gravitational Constant ($G$) Measurements
Standard physics treats the Newtonian gravitational constant $G$ as a universal constant, yet high-precision laboratory measurements (e.g., torsion balance experiments) stubbornly exhibit unresolvable scatter outside standard error margins.
- **SRC Prediction:** $G$ is not a fundamental constant of a static void; it is a localized measure of coupling efficiency to a planetary core's energetic sink. 
- **Testable Signatures:** Variations in $G$ will correlate directly with local plasma flux, telluric currents, and anisotropic variations in the local aether substrate density ($\rho_{\text{dfm}}$). Shielding an apparatus from local electromagnetic plasma interactions will alter the measured effective $G$ value.
