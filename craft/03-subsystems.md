# 03 - Subsystems

## 3.1 Plasma Field Modulation Array
The Plasma Field Modulation Array is the primary actuator for medium interaction, responsible for generating and vectoring the Lorentz and Eddy current sheets across the craft's outer skin.

- **Multi-Stage Coil Drivers:** High-frequency, pulsed-power electronics capable of driving superconducting windings embedded in the intermediate hull layer. These drivers modulate the magnetic field topology at megahertz to gigahertz frequencies to match the local harmonic structure of the aether substrate.
- **Gas-Dynamic Injection Rings:** Precision nozzles situated along the toroidal boundary that inject and ionize working gases (e.g., hydrogen-plasma mixes), maintaining a stable, low-friction boundary layer that interfaces directly with the Dynamic Fluid Medium (DFM).
- **Asymmetrical Vectoring Coils:** Segmented electromagnets that allow real-time shifting of the plasma sheath's density gradient, enabling directional steering without mechanical control surfaces or reactive mass expulsion.

## 3.2 Decoherence Control Units (DCUs)
The Decoherence Control Units manage the internal quantum-mechanical macro-states and regulate the vessel's potential energy state relative to the primary gravitational sink.

- **Resonant Cavity Oscillators:** High-Q microwave and scalar cavities that tune the internal core's neutral collective modes. By modulating the phase relationship between these modes and the surrounding DFM, the DCUs initiate controlled potential-well transitions.
- **Discharge Modulation Switches:** Ultrafast solid-state plasma switches that control the microsecond pulsing of energy into and out of the core condensate. This controls the rate of "sink detuning," ensuring smooth entry and exit from localized potential gradients.
- **Harmonic Suppression Filters:** Secondary feedback loops that absorb and redirect stray scalar and high-frequency energy spikes, preventing structural resonance fatigue in the surrounding hull matrix.

## 3.3 Navigational Feedback Sensors
Navigating a flowing aether substrate requires instruments capable of measuring medium-state properties rather than traditional inertial or GPS coordinates.

- **DFM Density Monitors:** High-precision laser interferometers configured to detect microscopic shifts in the local vacuum refractive index ($n_{\text{aether}}$), measuring adiabatic moduli drift in real time.
- **Phase-Lock Detectors:** Radio-frequency and scalar receivers tuned to monitor the harmonic signature of the planetary core's discharge, ensuring the craft maintains synchronization with the local aether flow.
- **Potential Gradient Sensors:** Differential field probes that map the vector and magnitude of the primary gravitational sink, identifying optimal streamlines and potential wells for medium-state steering.
